# DCI Enterprise — speaker notes

Use with `presentation/index.html`. Target length: about 12 minutes. Pause after slides 4, 8, and 13 for questions.

## 1 — Title

Open on the phrase “operating system,” not “app.” DCI already has a teacher client; this deck is about making that client the institute’s system of record.

## 2 — Agenda

Ask for one decision at the end: adopt as system of record, plus a one-batch pilot this term.

## 3 — The institute

Keep numbers honest. 2016, four programmes, four roles are grounded in the public site and the teacher app. Do not quote unverified “95% success” figures until the academic team signs them.

## 4 — The gap

Name the current pain without blaming teachers. Paper and WhatsApp are what every coaching class uses until someone owns the ledger.

## 5 — Definition

Four pillars: classroom, academics, people, trust. Trust is not a footnote — it is why this is “enterprise.”

## 6 — Product map

Walk the four roles. Emphasise assigned-class scoping for teachers. Directors should hear that they cannot casually create Admins from the Director role.

## 7 — Classroom

Daily loop: mark attendance → assign homework → write the daily report. That is the product’s heartbeat.

## 8 — Academics

A mark is institute property. PDF report cards and Excel import exist so the first term does not require retyping last year’s sheets.

## 9 — People

Read the table once. Then: no public email sign-up; staff are invited through a Cloud Function.

## 10 — Parents

WhatsApp is the parent portal in this market. Official sends must go through the server so tokens never sit in the APK.

## 11 — Trust

If a Class 10 mark sheet leaks, the brand leaks. Rules, functions, audit log, privacy pages.

## 12 — Architecture

This is not a mock. Flutter + Firebase + Next.js site + Play pipeline already exist in the DCI repos. Enterprise is the operating decision, not a greenfield rewrite.

## 13 — Roadmap

Resist fees and multi-branch until one batch has lived a full exam cycle on the platform.

## 14 — Close

Ask for: (1) system-of-record decision, (2) pilot batch, (3) director sign-off on roles and WhatsApp.
